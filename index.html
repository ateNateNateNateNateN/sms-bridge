Nathan Hatfield Personal SMS

Nathan Hatfield Personal SMS is a private, personal text messaging program run by Nathan Hatfield, an individual (sole proprietor). From his own mobile phone, Nathan texts his personal AI assistant at (949) 848-3569 and gets the assistant's replies by text.

Who receives messages

The only recipient is the operator, at his own mobile number. The program sends no marketing or promotional messages and does not text the public or anyone else.

How to opt in

To opt in, text START to (949) 848-3569. You will get this confirmation message:

Nathan Hatfield Personal SMS: You're subscribed to assistant replies. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

After you opt in, the assistant replies only to messages you send. There is no web sign-up form, and no numbers are added any other way.

What messages are sent

The messages are conversational replies from the assistant: answers, reminders you asked for, and task status updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP to (949) 848-3569 at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email nate.h@mail.grokbot.com.
